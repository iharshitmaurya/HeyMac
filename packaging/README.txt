FaceUnlock — unlock your Mac's lock screen and lock apps with your face, on Apple Silicon Macs.

INSTALL
1. Drag FaceUnlock into the Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS blocks it the first time because it is not from an identified
   developer: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message
   about FaceUnlock, click "Open Anyway", then confirm.
3. The setup window walks through camera access, enrolling your face and turning on the
   features you want.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Your face never leaves the Mac. It is stored encrypted, as numbers, not photos.
- Your password always keeps working. If your face is not recognized, macOS just asks
  for the password as usual.
- The camera runs only while a check is happening, and the camera light shows it.
- A photo or a face on a screen is rejected by the liveness check.

UNINSTALL
Menu bar icon > Settings… > Remove My Face Data, then quit FaceUnlock and drag it to
the Trash.
