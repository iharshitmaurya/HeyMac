Hey Mac 1.0.4

A clearer interface, easier setup, and more control when unlocking apps.

New interface

  • Redesigned settings with clear protection status and direct actions for permission and password issues.
  • Updated menu bar controls, animation replay, illustrated coverage choices, and website access.

Easier setup

  • Choose Lock Screen, App Lock, or both, then follow only the steps needed.
  • Select apps, finish later, cancel recognition, and review what is ready.
  • Your previous face enrollment stays in place until its replacement passes a recognition test.

Unlocking and reliability

  • Use Touch ID or your Mac password directly, stop recognition, or close an app without unlocking it.
  • Clearer relock timing, shorter Lock Screen scans, bounded retries, and improved no-face/no-match feedback.
  • Improved authentication cancellation and atomic encrypted-data saving.
  • Identity verification before sensitive face, password, and Lock Screen changes.

Compatibility

Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. This release is not notarized; fresh installations may require System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Existing users can choose Check for Updates… in Hey Mac. Automatic checks are available when enabled.
