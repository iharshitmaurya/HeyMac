## A clearer Hey Mac, from setup to unlock

### New interface
- Redesigned settings with Overview, Lock Screen, App Lock, Your Face, Appearance, and About pages.
- Consistent green protection indicators, clearer permission and password warnings, and direct actions to resolve setup problems.
- Updated menu bar controls for Lock Screen, App Lock, and pausing face recognition.
- Animation previews with a Replay button, illustrated App Lock coverage choices, and quick access to the Hey Mac website.

### Easier setup
- Choose Lock Screen, App Lock, or both, with only the setup steps needed for your choice.
- Select apps during setup, finish later, or cancel recognition and try again.
- Review what is ready before turning on protection.
- Replacing your face data now keeps the previous enrollment until the new setup passes its recognition test.

### Unlocking and reliability
- Choose Touch ID or your Mac password directly from the App Lock shield, stop face recognition, or close the protected app without unlocking it.
- Clearer relock timing and feedback when recognition does not see or match a face.
- Shorter Lock Screen scan windows and bounded retries reduce repeated camera scanning.
- Improved authentication cancellation handling and atomic saving of encrypted face and password data.
- Identity verification before saving a login password, replacing or removing face data, and turning off Lock Screen face unlock.

### Compatibility
- Requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later.
- This release is not notarized. If macOS blocks a fresh installation, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
- Existing users can use **Check for Updates…** in Hey Mac. Automatic update checks are available when enabled.

Validation: 107 automated tests pass, including checks across 2,048 protection-state combinations. Live camera, Touch ID, and multi-display behavior are not exhaustively covered by automated tests.
