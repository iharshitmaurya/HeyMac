## Daily activity now survives restarts

- The Lock Screen unlock count for today is saved locally and restored after Hey Mac or Mac restarts.
- The count resets on the next local day, even if Hey Mac stays open overnight.
- Recent Lock Screen activity remains visible after relaunch.
- App Lock authentications are not included in the Lock Screen count.

This release addresses [issue #6](https://github.com/iharshitmaurya/HeyMac/issues/6).
